# University of Waterloo Admissions Knowledge Base

Official source: https://uwaterloo.ca/future-students/

## Computer Science (Ontario applicants)

Ontario students must present **six Grade 12 U/M courses including**:

* English (ENG4U or equivalent)
* Advanced Functions (MHF4U)
* Calculus and Vectors (MCV4U)
* One other Grade 12 4U course

The remaining courses may be additional Grade 12 U/M courses of your choice so that you have at least six Grade 12 U/M courses in total.

Recommended: Grade 11 Introduction to Computer Science (ICS3U).

Admission is highly competitive and varies by year.

Waterloo states that admission decisions are based primarily on Grade 12 U/M courses and other factors such as the Admission Information Form (AIF) where applicable.

## Software Engineering

Required Grade 12 U/M courses:

* English (ENG4U)
* Advanced Functions (MHF4U)
* Calculus and Vectors (MCV4U)
* Chemistry (SCH4U)
* Physics (SPH4U)

Supplementary application required:

* Admission Information Form (AIF)
* Online interview

Software Engineering is one of Waterloo’s most competitive programs.

## General Waterloo admissions notes

* Ontario students apply through OUAC.
* Grade 11 marks may be used for early consideration when Grade 12 marks are not yet available.
* Final admission decisions are primarily based on Grade 12 U/M courses.
* Waterloo does not publish guaranteed admission cutoffs for these programs.
* Students should always verify requirements and deadlines on the official Waterloo website because they can change from year to year.
